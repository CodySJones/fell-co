# Fell & Co Website Plan

## Current Direction

Fell & Co is a straightforward residential interior design studio. The site should feel editorial, image-led, refined, and personal. It should not borrow any positioning from the prior Fell Concierge effort.

## Working Rule

Separate image decisions from page-structure decisions:

- Landing page imagery can feature a specific project, currently Crow's Perch Pool House.
- Portfolio structure should stay broad unless we are intentionally building or editing the Portfolio page.
- Project-specific galleries belong on future project detail pages, not in the home-page portfolio preview.

## Pages To Build

1. Home
   - Purpose: immediate brand impression and best visual work.
   - Current hero image: Crow's Perch Pool House.
   - Current sections: selected work, services, about/studio, journal preview, contact CTA.

2. About
   - Purpose: designer story, studio values, process tone, credibility.
   - Needed content: founder bio, studio philosophy, service area, portrait or studio imagery.

3. Portfolio
   - Purpose: full project index.
   - Needed content: project cards for Gallery House, Crow's Perch Pool House, Wakefield Kitchen, The A-Frame, and other Armitage projects we decide to reuse.

4. Services
   - Purpose: explain what clients can hire Fell & Co for.
   - Suggested services: interior design, renovation guidance, kitchen and bath, furnishings, whole-home design.

5. Contact
   - Purpose: project inquiry.
   - Needed content: inquiry form fields, email, phone, service area, expectation-setting copy.

6. Blog
   - Purpose: design journal and SEO support.
   - Suggested categories: Renovation Notes, Materials, Historic Homes, Furnishings, Project Stories.

## Next Best Step

After the home page direction is approved, build the pages one at a time in this order:

1. Portfolio
2. About
3. Services
4. Contact
5. Blog
