Crow’s Perch

Pool house remodel

The completed room brings together seating, kitchenette, pattern, color, and garden views into one bright, deeply personal retreat.

Project Story

A light-filled retreat for gathering, games, art, and plenty of margaritas.

Created for a young, creative couple preparing to start a family, Crow’s Perch transforms a dark, moody pool house into an inviting indoor-outdoor room. Nature, animals, entertaining, and eclectic design guided every layer.

The remodel opens the pool house to the outdoors, connects the grill to the kitchenette, and balances custom detail with durable materials suited for poolside living, pets, and future family life.

The pool house was reimagined as a bright indoor-outdoor retreat, opening the dark existing structure toward the pool and surrounding trees.

Design Notes

Nature, pattern, and everyday function shaped a room ready for new memories.

Every choice, from the operable doors to the crow mural, supports a pool house that feels cohesive, personal, and useful. It is a place for hosting friends, making art, playing games, and growing into family life.
